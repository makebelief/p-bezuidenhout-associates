CIVIL LITIGATION · GUIDE

Preparing for a Civil Dispute

What documents should you bring to a first consultation?

Disputes often begin with a misunderstanding or an unmet obligation.

Bring any agreements, written communications and a clear timeline of relevant events.

An attorney can explain potential remedies, applicable deadlines, cost considerations and whether settlement is suitable.

CONTRACTS & AGREEMENTS · GUIDE

Before Signing a Contract

Useful questions when reviewing a new agreement.

A contract creates legal responsibilities that can be difficult to change later.

Read the payment terms, duration, termination clauses and dispute resolution provisions carefully.

Seek tailored advice before signing when obligations are significant or unclear.

WILLS & ESTATES · GUIDE

Wills and Estate Planning Basics

Starting points for organising your wishes and records.

A clearly prepared will can help your loved ones understand your wishes.

Consider assets, dependants, beneficiaries, executors and any material changes in circumstances.

An attorney can advise on formalities required under South African law and the available planning options.
